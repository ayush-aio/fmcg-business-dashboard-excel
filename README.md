# FMCG Business Performance Dashboard (Excel)

##  Overview
This project focuses on analyzing FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) data to evaluate business performance across manufacturers, brands, and product attributes. The goal is to build an interactive dashboard that helps stakeholders track trends and make informed decisions.

---

##  Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- GETPIVOTDATA
- Slicers & Conditional Formatting

---

##  Data Preparation
- Cleaned raw dataset by removing null rows and restructuring headers
- Extracted manufacturer and brand information from product descriptions
- Eliminated aggregate rows to avoid duplication in analysis

---

##  Dashboard Features
- Interactive filtering using **slicers (package type, size, attributes)**
- Manufacturer-level performance tracking (2022, 2023, YTD 2024)
- Dynamic calculations using **GETPIVOTDATA**
- Average price computation (Value / Volume)

---

##  Key Analysis
- Evaluated **sales, volume, and pricing trends**
- Calculated **year-over-year growth rates (2022–2023)**
- Identified top-performing manufacturers and product segments
- Analyzed relationship between **price changes and demand**

---

##  Key Insights
- Market grew by **3.2%**, driven by price increase and stable demand
- Price increases often led to **lower sales (price sensitivity observed)**
- Certain brands achieved growth through **product innovation and pricing strategy**

---

##  Business Impact
- Enabled stakeholders to identify high-growth manufacturers and underperforming segments
- Provided insights into pricing strategies and market dynamics
- Supported data-driven decision-making through interactive reporting
